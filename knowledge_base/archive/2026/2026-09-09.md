### [Tiny molecular rings open new ways for designing better medicines](https://phys.org/news/2026-09-tiny-molecular-ways-medicines.html)


> Cyclopropanes are organic chemistry's smallest rings. Three carbon atoms are joined in a triangle, creating a compact and unusually strained structure.
